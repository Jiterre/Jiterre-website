Building the Ground Where Innovation Can Grow

Jiterre was founded on the belief that innovative medical technologies deserve a clear pathway to patients, clinicians, and healthcare systems.

Based in France and connected across Europe and Asia, Jiterre helps medical device companies understand markets, engage key stakeholders, identify strategic partners, and navigate the complexities of commercialization.

The name Jiterre reflects the idea of creating a place where ideas and ambitions can take root, grow, and create meaningful impact.

Services

Market Research

Analysis of market dynamics, competitors, reimbursement environments, and healthcare ecosystems.

Stakeholder Mapping

Identification and engagement strategies for healthcare professionals, hospital networks, industry associations, and key opinion leaders.

Partner Search

Identification and qualification of distributors, commercial partners, strategic collaborators, and local representatives.

Market Access & Regulatory Guidance

Support in connecting commercial, clinical, and regulatory considerations for successful market entry.

Business Development

Representation, strategic introductions, opportunity assessment, and growth support across France and Europe.
